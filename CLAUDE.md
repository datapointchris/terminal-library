# terminal-library

Notes on how command-line tools are driven, and the sequences worked out at the
terminal so they are not reassembled from scratch. There is no code here. `howto`
reads every file.

`README.md` is the one copy of every rule a change here follows. Read it before
editing a card, a Lab or the registry. It covers:

- what belongs in `cards/`, `labs/` and `tools/registry.yml`, and what stays out;
- what howto parses, where one bad front matter breaks the listing for every card;
- filenames as IDs, and the sweep before renaming one;
- the shape of a Lab, and running it end to end before committing it;
- the fields a registry entry carries, and when an entry is deleted;
- the cards' voice, and how a wrong claim is corrected;
- which lint and CI configs are generated, and when `.codespellrc` is edited.
